Quiche Math

Boundary: illustrative arithmetic only Labeled kitchen norms - 1 egg to half a cup of dairy, about a fifth of a cup per egg, about 4 cups in a 9-inch dish. Your recipe and your actual dish depth win every tie. This page never judges doneness or food safety; follow your recipe's bake guidance.

The ratio truth

The classic quiche custard is 1 egg to half a cup of dairy.

The batch truth

A large egg is about a fifth of a cup.

The pan truth

A 9-inch pie dish holds about 4 cups at the same depth; capacity scales with pan area.
