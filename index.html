Quiche Math

The recipe says bake until just set, and the ratio decided that before the oven did.

Your recipe and your actual dish depth win every tie. This page never judges doneness or food safety; follow your recipe's bake guidance.

The ratio truth

The classic quiche custard is 1 egg to half a cup of dairy. Four eggs and 2.0 cups of dairy pours 0.5 cups per egg - right at the classic ratio. Push past 0.6 and the wobble risks weeping; drop under 0.4 and it sets firmer and richer.

The batch truth

A large egg is about a fifth of a cup, so a 4-egg batch with 2.0 cups of dairy makes 2.8 cups of custard. Count the volume before you roll the crust, not while it sloshes.

The pan truth

A 9-inch pie dish holds about 4 cups at the same depth, and capacity scales with pan area - an 8-inch dish is rated about 3.2 cups, an 11-inch about 6.0. Your 2.8 cups fits the 9-inch with 1.2 cups to spare, and it would fill an 8-inch dish with only 0.4 cups to spare.
